# Dental emergencies

## Emergencies during opening hours
We keep two emergency slots every day, at 11:30 AM and 3:30 PM. An emergency exam costs 80 dollars.

## Emergencies when we are closed
Outside opening hours, call our after-hours line on 555 0199. For severe swelling, bleeding that will not stop, or trouble breathing, go to the nearest emergency room.

## What counts as an emergency
A knocked-out tooth, severe toothache, facial swelling, a broken tooth that hurts, or bleeding that will not stop are all emergencies.

## Medical advice
The receptionist cannot give medical advice or say what treatment a caller needs. For symptoms, offer an emergency slot so a dentist can look.
