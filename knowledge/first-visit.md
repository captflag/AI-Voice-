# Your first visit

## What to bring
Bring a photo ID, your insurance card, and a list of any medication you take. If you had dental X-rays in the last twelve months, bring those too.

## When to arrive
Please arrive 15 minutes before your first appointment to fill in the new patient forms. You can also complete them online from the link we text you.

## How long it takes
A first visit takes about one hour. It includes an exam and X-rays, and a cleaning if there is time.

## Before you come
Eat normally and brush as usual. There is no need to fast. If you take blood thinners, tell us before your appointment.

## Nervous patients
Tell us if you are anxious about dental treatment. We can book a longer slot and take breaks. Nitrous oxide, also called laughing gas, is available for 40 dollars.
