# Cancellations and lateness

## Cancelling or rescheduling
You can cancel or reschedule free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice.

## Late cancellations and missed appointments
Cancelling with less than 24 hours' notice, or not turning up, costs 30 dollars. The fee is waived the first time it happens.

## Running late
If you are more than 15 minutes late, we may have to reschedule your appointment.
