# Services and prices

## Routine cleaning
A routine cleaning costs 95 dollars. It takes about 45 minutes and includes scaling and a polish.

## Check-up and X-rays
A check-up with an exam costs 60 dollars. A check-up with bitewing X-rays costs 110 dollars. Adults are advised to have a check-up every six months.

## Fillings
A tooth-coloured filling starts at 150 dollars per tooth. The exact price depends on the size of the filling and is confirmed after an exam.

## Teeth whitening
In-clinic whitening costs 320 dollars for one 60-minute session. A take-home whitening kit costs 180 dollars.

## Root canals and crowns
A root canal starts at 700 dollars and a crown starts at 900 dollars. Both need a consultation first, and a written quote is given before any treatment.

## Children
A check-up for a child under 12 costs 45 dollars. A child's first visit before their third birthday is free.

## Treatments we do not offer
Bright Smile Dental does not offer braces, clear aligners or dental implants. For those we refer patients to Riverside Orthodontics.
