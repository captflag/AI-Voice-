# Hours and location

## Opening hours
The clinic is open Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM. The last appointment of the day starts at 4:30 PM. We are closed at weekends and on public holidays.

## Address
We are at 42 Maple Street, on the second floor, above Corner Pharmacy.

## Parking
There is free patient parking behind the building, with twelve spaces. Street parking on Maple Street is metered.

## Public transport
Bus routes 7 and 12 stop at Maple and Third, a two-minute walk from the clinic.

## Accessibility
The building has a lift, and the clinic is fully wheelchair accessible.
