# Insurance and payment

## Insurance plans we accept
We accept three insurance plans: Delta Dental, Cigna and MetLife. These are the only plans we accept, so we do not take Aetna, Medicare or any other insurer.

## How insurance billing works
We bill your insurer directly. You pay only your co-pay on the day of the visit. Please bring your insurance card to every appointment.

## Patients without insurance
Patients without insurance can join our membership plan for 25 dollars a month. It covers two cleanings and one check-up a year, and gives 15 percent off all other treatment.

## Ways to pay
We take credit and debit cards, cash, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We do not take personal cheques.

## Payment plans
For treatment costing more than 500 dollars, the bill can be split over up to six months with no interest.
